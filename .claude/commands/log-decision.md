---
description: Log an architectural or methodological decision to notes/DECISIONS.md
---

Append a new entry to notes/DECISIONS.md documenting a decision made this session.

Each entry must follow this format:

```
## [YYYY-MM-DD] <Short decision title>

**Context:** What problem or question prompted this decision.

**Decision:** What was decided.

**Rationale:** Why this option was chosen over alternatives.

**Alternatives considered:** Other options and why they were rejected.

**Affects:** Which phase(s) / files / components this touches.
```

Steps:
1. Read notes/DECISIONS.md to see existing entries and keep formatting consistent.
2. Ask the user (if not already clear from the conversation) what the decision, rationale, and alternatives were.
3. Append the new entry at the bottom of the file, dated with today's date.
4. Do not rewrite or reformat prior entries.
